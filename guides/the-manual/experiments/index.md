# Experiments

::: danger
USE AT YOUR OWN RISK
:::

Experiments are unstable features we are developing for potential inclusion in WarpDrive. Experimental features are PRE-RFC and are not guaranteed to make it into WarpDrive.

- [Storage Resources](./storage-resources.md): reactive class fields persisted to `localStorage`
  or `sessionStorage`.
- [Pagination](./pagination.md): reactive state for paginated collections, and the `<Paginate />`
  and `<EachLink />` components.
