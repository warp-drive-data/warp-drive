# ***Warp*Drive** Blog

Release announcements and other point-in-time posts, dual-published here so that non-Ember
consumers of ***Warp*Drive*** don't have to go looking for them on
[blog.emberjs.com](https://blog.emberjs.com/).

Like [Upgrading](/upgrading/), posts here have permanent URLs: a post's URL is never renamed and
never unpublished, and every post is tagged with the version and date it was written for. Posts
are organized into sub-sections by major version.

See [Upgrading and Blog Pages](/guides/contributing/writing-documentation/writing-guides.md#upgrading-and-blog-pages)
for the authoring rules that keep these guarantees true.

## Major Versions

- [5.x](/blog/v5/)
